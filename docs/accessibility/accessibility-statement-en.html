Accessibility Statement

İzmir Institute of Technology (İYTE) is committed to making its institutional website accessible to the widest possible audience. This statement describes the accessibility status of our website at iyte.edu.tr.

Conformance Status

Our website conforms to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level A criteria. A substantial portion of Level AA criteria are also met; however, some legacy content and third-party embedded elements may present limited non-conformance.

Accessibility Features

  • A "Skip to main content" link is available at the top of each page so keyboard users can bypass repetitive navigation.
  • All icon-only links (social media, search, menu) carry screen-reader–readable labels.
  • Keyboard focus is always indicated by a visible outline.
  • The homepage news carousel ("manşet") supports keyboard control, provides a pause/play button, and respects the "reduce motion" setting at the operating-system level.
  • The page structure uses semantic regions: header, navigation, main content and footer landmarks.
  • The site can be fully navigated using a keyboard alone; a pointing device is not required.
  • The site is available in Turkish and English; the appropriate lang attribute is set per page.

Known Limitations

We are aware of the following limitations and are working to address them:

  • Legacy images: Some images uploaded before 2018 may lack alternative text. Our content team is auditing existing pages and adding descriptions where missing.
  • PDF documents: Certain PDF documents published on the site (such as policy documents) may not include accessibility tags. To request an accessible version, please contact us using the details below.
  • Third-party content: Embedded Google Maps, video players and external service widgets are outside the scope of our accessibility control.
  • Colour contrast: Some text/background combinations may fall below the Level AA contrast threshold due to corporate brand colours. These instances are being identified and remediated.
